Where this game comes from

This is a class lab exercise that reskins and rebuilds the classic browser game "Whack Your Ex", originally created by Tom Winkler (developer handle Doodieman), first released as a Flash game in 2004 alongside its sibling Whack Your Boss. All credit for the original concept — a cartoon ex-partner, a tray of everyday objects, and a silly animated "whack" for each one — belongs to Tom Winkler.

This version is a from-scratch, non-commercial fan build made for a school assignment. It uses new artwork, new copy, and a lighter, cartoon-only tone with no gore, drawn purely from the original game's core mechanic. It isn't affiliated with or endorsed by Tom Winkler or Doodieman.

Learn about the original: the Internet Archive preserves the original Flash release, and Doodieman's other games (including Whack Your Boss) are documented on flash-game archive sites.
